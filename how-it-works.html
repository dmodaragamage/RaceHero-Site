HOW IT WORKS

A coach,
not a dashboard.

RaceHero rides with you: cues in your ear while you drive, a debrief that explains why after, and a Viewer where every claim can be scrubbed with your finger. Here is one session, end to end — every number on this page comes from it.

Status: Google Play — internal testing · racehero.ca

One session, five screens

The app is built around a single session's story — before, during, and after. No setup theatre: the path is download, drive, debrief.

Plug it in, data, and it's done.

01 — HOME

Your racing, as a story

Home opens on your last session — the headline lap, your perfect lap built from your own best sectors, and the one corner the coach wants to work on. Not a menu of features: a story with a next chapter.

02 — PRE-SESSION

Green means go

Before you roll, the Live Data Strip streams every channel in plain words — WAITING, WORKING, LIVE, or honestly stalled. If a channel isn't real, the app says so before it can coach from it, not after.

  • No calibration wizards — RaceHero Link auto-maps your pedals and wheel
  • Pre-GO driving is never counted as session data
03 — LIVE

The coach in your ear

An in-seat voice instructor that remembers lap to lap: it learns your brake and turn-in points, references your last lap at this corner, and explains corrections as causes, not slogans. On screen: the last lap, big; sector dots in green, red, amber; your feet as travel bars — never a percentage, because a % promises calibration precision that isn't real.

04 — DEBRIEF

A debrief, not a data dump

The debrief reads like a conversation with your instructor: what I saw, the moment that mattered, the one thing, one drill. The full lap table and engineering numbers sit one tap below — verdicts first, evidence attached, every claim tied to a metre of track.

05 — VIEWER + CORNER SCRUB

Scrub any corner with your finger

The session on one screen: the road as a road, your line against your composite, sectors and corners you can tap. Tap a corner and it opens; open the Corner Scrub and drag — the data tracks your finger through the turn, with a verdict stamp the data has to earn: HIT, BRAKED EARLY, TOO WIDE. No measurement, no stamp.

Checked against the ground

A coach that can't be checked is just an opinion. RaceHero's targets come from a physics solver fed with real terrain profiles — elevation sampled from named survey data — and we grade the solver against the ground before its numbers reach a driver.

⛰️

Terrain is the ground truth

Every circuit profile carries its source. On a downhill braking zone the ground itself changes how late you can brake — a flat-earth model can't know that, so ours isn't one. Where no profile exists yet, the app says "flat model" on the label. Never silently.

🧪

We test it, on the record

In a verification run on this session, feeding the solver a labelled 8% descent into Turn 4 moved its brake point from 2,241.5 m to 2,215.4 m — 26 m earlier, the physically correct direction (a descent costs ~0.78 m/s² of braking). A control corner didn't move at all. That's the standard every coaching number has to meet.

🧩

Your best pieces, stitched

RaceHero builds a composite reference from your own best sectors — the perfect lap your driving already contains, labelled yours — best pieces wherever it appears. The coach measures you against your best self first; physics targets sit alongside, never disguised as something you drove.

Every reference says where it came from

No mystery lines. Each reference in RaceHero carries its provenance, in the open:

REAL — SHARED DATA

A line driven by a real car, shared with the pool. The fastest honest benchmark there is.

YOURS — BEST PIECES

Your own best segments, stitched across your laps and sessions. Achievable — because you already did every piece of it.

TARGET — COACH'S FIX

The one change the coach is asking for at this corner, bounded by what physics says your grip allows.

THEORETICAL — PHYSICS

A solved lap: physics, calibrated to your demonstrated grip, fed with real terrain. A bound, not a promise.
