When a channel goes quiet

Mid-session, the connection card names it in a sentence — never a silent freeze, never a percentage:

Your pedals went quiet — check RaceHero Link is still running on your PC.
The game feed stopped — check the game is still running and sending telemetry.
GPS went quiet — it comes back on its own with a clear view of the sky.
Lap timing went quiet — keep driving; it re-locks at the start/finish line.

And if the whole feed stops moving, the strip at the top of this screen says so in red: Stopped — the feed went quiet. (Use the LIVE STATES buttons in the index to see it.)

Twenty seconds into a session with no data at all, the coach says it out loud, once: "I don't have your data yet. Check the PC Link shows connected — I'll start coaching the moment it arrives."

THE GARAGE

Build the 25 Hz puck.

The DIY GPS puck RaceHero was born with. Plain instructions, real parts — build it on the bench, and the app finds it by itself.

The wiring, in words

GPS module's transmit wire goes to the radio's receive pin. Power comes from the battery rail — red to positive, black to ground. The antenna faces the sky, always: a puck mounted upside-down is a puck that lies to you politely.

Fix rate25 Hz — set in the module, kept by the badge
Talks to the phone byBluetooth — NMEA or the module's own fast protocol
Cost of the partsabout a tank of fuel
WHEN IT'S BUILT

Nothing. That's the point. Power it on near the phone and it appears in the hub — remembered, reconnected, working. Zero setup is the feature.
