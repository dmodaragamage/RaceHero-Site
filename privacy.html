RACEHERO — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

RaceHero is built to keep your data yours. There is no account, no analytics SDK, no advertising, and no data broker. What happens on your phone stays on your phone.

Location

Precise location is used for lap timing, track detection, and mapping your start/finish line. Location data is processed on-device to time your laps and is stored only in your on-device session history. It is never transmitted to us.

Sessions & telemetry

Lap times, GPS trails, and telemetry are stored locally on your device so you can review past sessions. You can delete them at any time from within the app.

Voice coaching

Spoken coaching uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. No audio is recorded or sent anywhere by this feature.

AI Debrief (optional)

If you use the AI Debrief, an anonymized summary of your session (lap times, sector deltas, corner statistics — no location trail, no personal identifiers) is sent to Google Gemini, Google’s AI service, through RaceHero’s own secure server, to generate your coaching report. The app itself never holds an AI key and never talks to Google directly. This only happens when you explicitly request a debrief.

SIM racing

SIM mode communicates over your local Wi-Fi network between the app and the RaceHero Link PC companion. Telemetry never leaves your local network except as described under AI Debrief, and only at your request.

Permissions

  • Location — lap timing and track detection.
  • Notifications — the ongoing-recording indicator while a session runs.
  • Bluetooth — labeling your audio route (e.g. connected glasses); never for tracking.

Children

RaceHero is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
