A coach that learns you

The physics is universal. The coaching is personal — because the engine calibrates itself to the only driver it coaches: you. All of this runs on your phone, on your data, today.

1

It remembers your laps

The coach carries every corner's last-lap result into the next lap. "Later than last lap — you braked early again" is a comparison against your driving, not a textbook.

2

It learns your points

Your brake points and turn-in points are learned from your own fastest laps — where you actually brake, not where a formula guesses. Your reference lap is built from your best sectors, and targets are set at what you've demonstrably done, never at your one luckiest moment.

3

It explains the why

Every debrief starts with the cause — the input that cost the time, at the corner where it happened — drawn from a coaching library built on real coaching. Numbers come second. Verdicts before measurements, always.
