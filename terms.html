The short version

RaceHero is a lap-timing and coaching tool. It helps you drive better; it does not drive for you, and it never replaces your own judgment, your instructor, or the rules of your track or series.

The service

RaceHero™ is an Android app for karting, track days and sim racing, currently in internal testing ahead of its public release on Google Play, together with this website and the free RaceHero Link companion app for Windows. Features may change as the product develops.

Safety first

  • Never interact with your phone while driving. Anything you need to touch happens in the pit lane.
  • Coaching cues are suggestions from recorded data, not instructions. If a cue and the corner in front of you disagree, the corner wins.
  • You are responsible for complying with your venue's, sanctioning body's and insurer's rules on devices, mounts and cameras.
