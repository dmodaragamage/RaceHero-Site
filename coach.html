How she coaches

Every session starts the same way — her contract with you: Lap 1 I show you the track. Lap 2 we speed up. Lap 3 I coach.

1

Lap 1 — she shows you the track

A guided lap at pace. As each corner comes up, Lena walks you through it — where to brake, where to turn in, what the corner asks of the car. No stopwatch pressure yet; just learn the lap.

2

Lap 2 — we speed up

The training wheels come off in stages. Lena steps the pace up and starts watching your inputs — your real throttle and brake — against the lines you just learned.

3

Lap 3 — she coaches

Full coaching, live. Two focus corners per lap, never a flood: the brake point that's drifting, the corner that's costing you, calm words when it goes wrong. Afterwards, the debrief names the one thing to fix next.

Talk to Lena.

One coach, two registers. In the Film Room she teaches — in the car, she rides with you. Either way, you don't dig through menus or data tables. You just ask.
