Designed and printed here, not drop-shipped

The housing is ours end to end: designed by RaceHero's founder, printed in-house as a two-part shell, and closed with screws into heat-set inserts. If a battery ever tires or a board ever needs swapping, the puck opens, gets fixed, and goes back on the kart — no sealed, disposable design, and no generic enclosure with a sticker on it. Every puck leaves the same bench: assembled, tested, and flashed with the latest firmware before it ships.

How it works with RaceHero™

  1. Power it on trackside — the puck starts broadcasting its 25Hz stream.
  2. Open the RaceHero™ app — PuckLink finds it over Bluetooth and badges it as your puck.
  3. Drive. The app reads every fortieth of a second: lap timing to the hundredth, live delta against your optimal lap, and corner-by-corner telemetry for the AI coach.
