How it works

  1. Power it on and give it a view of the sky.
  2. Drive. The BL-1000GT logs 10 Hz GNSS to its microSD on board — no phone needed.
  3. Review in QRacing. Qstarz’s own app and PC software open the logs. Its Bluetooth link speaks Qstarz’s proprietary protocol — not NMEA — so RaceHero does not connect to this device; for a receiver RaceHero reads live, choose a “Tested with RaceHero™” pick on the gear page.
