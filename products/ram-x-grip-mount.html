How it works with RaceHero™

  1. Clamp the U-bolt base to your kart's steering column or Nassau panel rail and tighten the nyloc nuts.
  2. Drop your phone in the X-Grip — the spring arms snap it in place; loop the tether for insurance.
  3. Run the RaceHero™ app with the live HUD and voice coaching. Your phone is now your dash.
