How it connects — honestly. Despite the name, this adapter talks to RaceHero over classic Bluetooth on Android: it is a dual-mode device, and classic Bluetooth is the OBD-II path the app uses — RaceHero does not use Bluetooth Low Energy for OBD-II. What you are paying for versus the no-name clones is a genuine ELM327 chip that holds a stable link all session instead of dropping mid-lap. If you want the fastest possible PID rate, step up to the OBDLink MX+.

How it works with RaceHero™

  1. Plug it into the OBD-II port under the dash — it powers up on its own.
